Existing generic products connect to proposed Pen, Pen Part, and Refill detail tables through shared primary keys. Pen parts provide mechanism, actuator, tip, top-cap, and clip roles. Refill products contain tip variants and color offerings, and compatibility facts connect pens and optional tips to compatibility groups or exact refill products. Existing collection items connect to proposed Pen and Pen Part collection detail tables. Installed parts may be virtual selections or links to explicitly owned part collection items.
